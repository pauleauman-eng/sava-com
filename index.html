Who We Are

About SAVA.COM

SAVA.COM stands for Smoking, Vaping, and Alcohol Addiction.

SAVA.COM is an educational website developed as a student project to promote awareness of the harmful effects of smoking, vaping, and alcohol misuse.

Our goal is to provide accessible information, encourage responsible decision-making, and help individuals understand the importance of prevention, healthier habits, and recovery support.

Through educational articles, practical guides, quizzes, motivational resources, and a personal progress tracker, SAVA.COM encourages visitors to make informed decisions for their health and well-being.

💚

Our Mission

To educate, inspire, and empower individuals—especially young people— with knowledge about the risks of smoking, vaping, and harmful alcohol use.

Our Vision

A more informed, health-conscious community where people are encouraged to make responsible choices and seek help when needed.
