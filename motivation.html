Small Steps, Real Progress

Healthy Actions You Can Try

Small positive actions can support your well-being as you work toward healthier habits.

💧

Drink Water

Have a glass of water and give yourself a moment to refocus.

🚶

Move Your Body

Try a short walk or gentle stretching, if it's safe and comfortable for you.

💬

Talk to Someone

Reach out to a trusted friend, family member, counselor, or healthcare professional.

📝

Write It Down

Notice what you're feeling and identify one positive action you can take next.

🎨

Enjoy a Hobby

Spend a little time on an activity that helps you feel engaged and supported.

🎯

Choose one manageable step instead of worrying about everything at once.
