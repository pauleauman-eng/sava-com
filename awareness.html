Knowledge Is Prevention

Understand the Risks. Protect Your Health.

Smoking, vaping, and harmful alcohol use can affect many parts of the body. Explore the medical illustrations below to understand possible health consequences and how healthier choices can help.

Health Awareness

Three Habits, Serious Health Risks

Learning how these substances affect the body is an important step toward prevention and informed decision-making.

Smoking

Cigarette smoke exposes the body to nicotine, carbon monoxide, and many toxic chemicals.

Smoking can damage the lungs, blood vessels, and heart, and increases the risk of several cancers and chronic diseases.

Explore Effects

Vaping

Electronic cigarettes produce an aerosol that users inhale into their lungs.

This aerosol may contain nicotine, ultrafine particles, metals, and other potentially harmful substances. Vaping is not risk-free.

Explore Effects

Alcohol

Alcohol affects the central nervous system and can impair coordination, attention, and judgment.

Heavy or prolonged drinking can contribute to liver disease, heart problems, certain cancers, and alcohol dependence.

Explore Effects
Smoking Awareness

Effects of Smoking on the Body

Cigarette smoking can harm organs throughout the body, not only the lungs.

Possible Health Consequences

🫁 Lungs

Smoking increases the risk of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), lung cancer, and respiratory problems.

❤️ Heart and Blood Vessels

Smoking increases the risk of coronary heart disease, stroke, and blood vessel damage.

🧠 Brain

Nicotine is addictive, while smoking increases the risk of stroke.

🦷 Mouth and Teeth

Tobacco use increases the risk of gum disease, tooth loss, and cancers of the mouth.

Vaping Awareness

Effects of Vaping on the Body

Vaping exposes the lungs to aerosol containing substances that may harm respiratory health.

Possible Health Consequences

🫁 Respiratory System

E-cigarette aerosol may irritate the airways and contribute to inflammatory changes in the lungs.

🧠 Nicotine Dependence

Many vaping products contain nicotine, which can lead to addiction and affect the developing adolescent brain.

🧪 Chemical Exposure

Aerosol can contain metals, ultrafine particles, and potentially harmful chemicals.

⚠️ Lung Injury

Severe vaping-associated lung injury has occurred, particularly during outbreaks involving certain THC-containing products.

Alcohol Awareness

Effects of Alcohol on the Body

Alcohol affects multiple organs. Risks depend on the amount consumed, drinking patterns, and individual health.

Possible Health Consequences

🧠 Brain

Alcohol can affect memory, coordination, judgment, and decision-making.

❤️ Heart

Heavy drinking can contribute to high blood pressure, irregular heart rhythms, and heart muscle damage.

🩺 Liver

Long-term harmful alcohol use can cause fatty liver, hepatitis, fibrosis, and cirrhosis.

⚠️ Dependence and Withdrawal

Alcohol dependence may lead to withdrawal symptoms. Severe withdrawal can be medically dangerous.

Prevention and Healthy Living

Six Steps Toward Healthier Choices

Building healthy habits and seeking trustworthy support can help reduce substance-related risks.

1

Learn the Facts

Use reliable health information to understand the risks of substance use.

2

Recognize Triggers

Identify situations, stressors, and routines associated with cravings or risky habits.

3

Choose Healthy Activities

Make time for exercise, hobbies, rest, and supportive social connections.

4

Build Support

Talk with trusted family members, friends, counselors, or healthcare professionals.

5

Set Realistic Goals

Take manageable steps and recognize progress without expecting perfection.

6

Seek Professional Help

Ask for medical advice or treatment when substance use is difficult to control or withdrawal is possible.

Move Forward

Knowledge Is the First Step

Continue learning, explore recovery guidance, or find professional support when needed.
